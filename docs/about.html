About

A daily paper for one fantasy basketball league.

The Goon Gazette covers Ballinwithmygoons, a 12-team, 11-category head-to-head Yahoo league. Every morning it reads the league's data, runs the numbers and publishes the damage: last night's box scores, this week's fights, playoff odds, waiver grades, an all-play Gauntlet, and a Monday Weekly Wrap told as a story. During games it updates hourly. The commentary is written by an AI beat writer with one editorial rule: expose every manager's atrocities, and never invent a stat.

Built and run by Parth, one of the twelve managers, who would like it on the record that the paper roasts his team the same as everyone else's.

Say something

Bugs, ideas, trash talk, a stat the paper got wrong. It goes straight to Parth's inbox.

How the paper works

Morning editionBy 6am ET: Last Night, This Week, Victory Road, Waivers and The Gauntlet, rebuilt from the night's box scores.
TonightHourly from 7pm to 1am ET while games are on: live matchup totals, lead changes and the best lines so far.
Weekly WrapEvery Monday, the week that was, written as one story in a rotating theme.
Picks and the Trade BlockManagers put predictions on the record and shop players to the league. Both are honor system.
The numbersPlayoff odds from 2,000 simulations of the remaining schedule; player values are z-scores across all 11 categories; nothing in the prose is made up.
Reader messages are logged and emailed to the editor. No cookies, no tracking beyond a pageview count.Fantasy data provided by Yahoo Fantasy
